# Camilo Cáceres

Staff ML Engineer at MercadoLibre. I build ML systems for Display Ads — bidding, attribution, and GenAI at scale.

PhD in Optimization of Systems (UNICAMP, Brazil). Former mechatronics engineer. Still applying control theory to ML problems that others can't formulate.

## What I Do

At MercadoLibre, I lead ML systems for Display Ads. Not models in notebooks — systems that handle billions of impressions daily, with latency budgets measured in milliseconds. The work spans bidding optimization, pacing algorithms, attribution, and GenAI for content moderation.

Before MELI, I spent 6 years at UNICAMP (Brazil) doing a PhD in optimization, control theory, and neuroevolution. I built controllers for autonomous robots using genetic algorithms and potential fields. I still use that mindset — system dynamics, feedback loops, stability analysis — to design ML systems that don't collapse under load.
